Entry for make.fun's "The Meeting That Should Have Been One Line" bounty ($2, winner takes all).

The entry is one sentence that could have replaced an entire pointless meeting — it IS the meeting's outcome: the verdict (launch Friday, budget approved) plus a rule that kills future pointless meetings, with the sting landing on the very meeting it replaces.

> Approved: launch Friday, budget $40k, and from today every meeting must fit in one sentence or it doesn't happen, starting with this one.

Run `python3 main.py` with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set; it writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (137 characters, no trailing newline).
